POLARIS - AI mix & master assistant (early test build)

INSTALL
1. Copy "Polaris.vst3" to   ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
   Copy "Polaris.component" (Audio Unit, for Logic/GarageBand) to   ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
2. This build is not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS will block it at first.
   Open Terminal and run:
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Polaris.vst3
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Polaris.component
3. Rescan plugins in your DAW and load Polaris last on the master/stereo out.

QUICK TEST WITHOUT A DAW
Open Polaris.app (right-click > Open the first time).

FIRST STEPS
- Choose a Target (Streaming -14, Loud -9, Club -7 LUFS) and a Style.
- Play the loudest 20-30 seconds of your song and press Listen.
- Apply the suggestions that sound right. Ride Drive for more glue and loudness.
- Use Compare to hear the original mix at the same loudness.

Source code and updates: https://github.com/davepomerlo/polaris
